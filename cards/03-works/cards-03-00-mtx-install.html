Element X is a free chat app on Matrix, an open chat network that no single company owns. Any business can run a chat address there, and customers reach it without a phone number or an ad-funded platform.

Four steps, about five minutes. The business address used below, @business:matrix.org, is an example: use the address the business gives you.

why Matrix: agent to agent on both sides

Matrix is an open protocol that welcomes bots, and a bot joins a room the same way a person does. That makes it the place to test, develop and run A2A (agent to agent) for free, today.

And not only for the merchant. The buyer can run a chatbot too, enhanced by an AI of its choice and managed from its own dashboard. The client asks their bot once, in plain words. Their bot talks to the merchant bot in a Matrix room, and the merchant bot brings in the merchant. Human chat keeps working on the same network the whole time, so a person on either side can step in at any moment.

1. install Element X

Search for Element X - Secure Chat & Call in Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone) and install it. Pick Element X, not "Element Classic".

Google Play · App Store

2. create a free account with Google

  • Open Element X and tap Create account. The account provider is already set to matrix.org: tap Continue.
  • On Create an account, tap Continue with Google and pick a Google account. No new password to remember.
  • Choose a Username. It becomes your chat address, for example @yourname:matrix.org.
  • Tick I agree to the Terms and Conditions and tap Create Account. On Continue to Element X? tap Continue to go back to the app.

If the app offers Set up recovery, do it and keep the recovery key somewhere safe, such as a password manager. It is the only way to read old chats after signing in on another phone.

3. find the business

On Chats, tap the green + at the bottom right and type the business address exactly, including the @ and the colon: @business:matrix.org

Tap the business in the results, then tap Send invite.

4. say hello

Send hello. The first message can take about 15 seconds, because the business's chat service has to accept your invite first. After that, an automatic reply usually arrives within seconds, and a person at the business reads every chat.

good to know

  • Same account everywhere: sign in on a computer at app.element.io with the same Google account.
  • No phone number shared: the business sees only your chat address and the name you chose.
  • Encrypted: private chats on Element X are end-to-end encrypted.
