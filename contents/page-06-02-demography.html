We don't just sell laptops. We sell a service that extends the life of your IT devices, and the know-how to get every bit of computing power out of them.

To us, it's the same either way. A high-end 2nd hand laptop or a mediocre brand-new one on the same budget: we know how to push both to their full potential. Even if you go for a cheaper, mediocre 2nd hand device, we know exactly how to get the most out of it, limits and all.

That is what we sell.

Break the flow and change the habit: switch to open-source operating systems and software. If the old way of working isn't taking your business anywhere, you need a new way to get things done. 2nd hand hardware gives your business a genuine refresh. Give a device a second chance, and the "business fatigue" of bloated, standard systems clears out with it. Shake up the software with a complete open-source stack, and the weight of the old workflow goes too. That is what refreshing with old hardware really means.

If your business is going through a transformation, or training staff for a new chapter, low-risk 2nd hand hardware paired with open-source systems is a smart bet. It is the smartest way to bring open source into your business processes, from productivity suites to ERPs. 2nd hand laptops and mini-PCs let you build self-contained workflows, or give a specific task its own dedicated machine, without the high cost of new equipment. A low-cost entry into a high-performance way of working.

So, who is the buyer of 2nd hand hardware?

  • The Power User: knows exactly what these machines can do, and wants an open-source environment that pushes the hardware to its absolute limit.
  • The Urgent Need: is in a dire situation and needs working tech right now. We get the device running, without the "brand-new" price tag.
  • The Deal Seeker: wants the best value. We are the conduit to hardware sold "as-is" at a fair price. We take no cut and make no profit on the hardware; we only charge upfront processing costs, to ensure quality and delivery.
